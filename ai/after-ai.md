# After AI: Reasoning from History

<p class="experiment-date">November 2026 · Series of two</p>

Predictions about AI tend to start from the technology and work forwards. I wanted to try the opposite: give AI a large question about the future and ask it to reason from first principles and from what earlier technological revolutions actually did, then project forward only once the pattern was clear.

Each piece follows the same method. Start from a brief and a set of beliefs, treat them as hypotheses rather than facts, look back across history for evidence, extract rules, test those rules against what AI has done so far, and only then predict. Every prediction carries a confidence level and a test that would show it to be wrong.
