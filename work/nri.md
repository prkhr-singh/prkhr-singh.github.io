## Battery-as-a-service market entry strategy

**Period:** 2018 to 2023  
**Markets:** India, Southeast Asia  
**Skills:** market sizing, commercial feasibility, customer segmentation, go-to-market planning

**What prompted the work.** I worked on questions about whether battery-as-a-service models could be commercially viable in new markets.

**The problem.** The strategy had to connect market demand, customer needs, pricing, operations, and partner choices rather than treating adoption as a single forecast.

**What I did.** I contributed to market sizing, feasibility and profitability analysis, customer segmentation, and go-to-market framing. I compared alternative business-model assumptions and translated research into choices about target users, pricing, and rollout.

**Outputs.** Market models, research decks, commercial scenarios, and strategy recommendations.

**Impact or learning.** This work taught me to test business-model assumptions against operating realities. A promising technology needs a clear customer use case and a credible route to repeatable economics.

**Reach out when.** You need to assess a battery-as-a-service market · You need to connect demand, unit economics, and launch choices

## Electric three-wheeler battery-swap pilot support

**Period:** 2020 to 2021  
**Markets:** India  
**Skills:** pilot planning, partner coordination, operating reviews, issue tracking

**What prompted the work.** I supported a battery-swapping pilot that needed coordination between the vehicle, charging, station, and operating workstreams.

**The problem.** A pilot could only produce useful learning if readiness, daily operations, exceptions, and partner responsibilities were tracked together.

**What I did.** I supported the project-management rhythm, including timelines, partner coordination, resource planning, reporting, and review of operational issues. I helped organise the evidence into a clear view of what the pilot tested and what remained unresolved.

**Outputs.** Pilot plans, operating trackers, partner updates, and a final report.

**Impact or learning.** I learned that pilots are as much about disciplined observation and issue resolution as technical performance. Clear ownership and a shared operating record make later scale decisions more credible.

**Reach out when.** You need to move a mobility concept into a field pilot · You need a practical pilot-management rhythm across partners

**Period:** 2019  
**Markets:** India  
**Skills:** qualitative research, ethnography, customer-journey synthesis, insight translation

**What prompted the work.** I worked on research into how drivers used vehicles and digital services in everyday life.

**The problem.** Feature ideas needed to be grounded in motivations, habits, and pain points that conventional product assumptions could miss.
