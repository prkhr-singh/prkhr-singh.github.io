## What AI Got Wrong / Missed

**Missed — flip hypothesis:** The AI had no way to know the purchase/renovation timeline: bought Sept 2025, renovated Jan 2026, listed May 2026 — 8-month ownership cycle. Agent's stated reason ("upsizing; needs vehicle storage") is inconsistent with 8-month ownership. This reads as a renovation-for-resale (flip). The renovation is designed to appeal to buyers, not to live in. Cosmetic quality does not guarantee structural quality underneath.

**Missed — split-system-only heating as a functional negative:** The AI expected average kitchen storage and did not flag heating as a concern. In person, the absence of ducted heating/cooling in a Geelong property (cold winters) is a meaningful gap. This is a self-funded post-purchase cost.

**Missed — kitchen range hood quality:** The very basic chimney/range hood was specifically flagged as requiring upgrade. This was not apparent from listing photos.

**Overestimated — renovation quality signal:** A recent renovation (bathroom within 12 months) would normally be a positive signal. In a flip context, it becomes a risk signal. The AI cannot distinguish between "genuine upgrade" and "cosmetic cover."
