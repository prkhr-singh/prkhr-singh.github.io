## Split-Level Build Type — Research Notes (9 May 2026)

Split-level homes in Australia:
- **Natural temperature zoning advantage:** Lower level stays cooler in summer; reduces need for constant cooling. But upper-level sections heat up quickly in summer without good insulation or cross-ventilation.
- **Heating challenge:** Uneven temperature distribution is the main complaint. Standard ducted systems often struggle to balance a split-level properly — multiple zones or additional split-systems are typically needed.
- **Insulation is critical:** Proper roof, wall, and floor insulation can cut heating/cooling costs by 50%+ in Australian homes. Without it, energy bills suffer in a multi-level design.
- **Resale:** Mixed outcomes. Design-led buyers price it as a premium; traditional buyers see it as unusual. In a gentrifying suburb like Highton (lifestyle-move buyer profile increasing), design character increasingly sells.
- **Renovation limitations:** Split-level layouts are harder to modify than simple single or double storey. Major structural changes require engineering input. Internal modifications are possible; reconfiguring levels is not.

**For 11 Northbridge specifically:** Verify heating/cooling setup before offering. If there's no ducted reverse cycle, budget $8–15k for a multi-zone split-system installation or ducted upgrade — this is a real cost that offsets the lower land price vs comparables.

Sources: [Unique Built — Split-Level Pros & Cons](https://uniquebuilt.com.au/pros-cons-split-level-homes/), [The Good Builder — Split-Level Energy](https://thegoodbuilder.com.au/split-level-homes-how-aussies-are-turning-to-smarter-design-to-cut-energy-bills/)
