## What's Not Working — Ranked by Severity

### CRITICAL

**1. Subsidence — Major Defect Across All Areas**

This is the most important finding in the report. The inspector used a laser level to measure floor levels and found subsidence affecting the entire main building.

- Classified as a **Major Defect** under AS 4349.1 — rectification is required to avoid unsafe conditions, loss of utility, or further deterioration
- Location: All areas (not isolated)
- Evidence observed: Floor undulation, gapping to eave linings, cracks adjacent to windows, footpath movement, decks out of level
- Cause: Foundation movement from changes in soil moisture content
- Inspector's recommendation: Consult a **structural engineer** to scope works; possibly also a **geotechnical engineer** if soil moisture is the ongoing cause; works will likely involve some form of underpinning/re-stumping
- Remediation quote: Vendor obtained a quote from Surf Coast Underpinning (visible on page 10 of report) — amount partially obscured but appears to be approximately **$14,000 including GST**
- Critical unknown: Whether the subsidence is **historical and stable** (e.g., one-off drainage event now resolved) or **ongoing** (active soil movement). If ongoing, the $14k quote may significantly understate the true cost

The B&P was commissioned by the vendor (Mathew Hood) in March 2026 — meaning the vendor has known about this defect since at least March. The vendor elected to provide this report rather than remediate before listing.

**3. S32 Vendor Statement Is Out of Date**

The S32 was signed by both vendors on **22 September 2025**. The underlying Register Search Statement is dated **2 September 2025**.

Under the Sale of Land Act, if the Register Search is more than **6 months old** when the contract is signed, the S32 must be updated. Six months from 2 September 2025 = **2 March 2026**. The S32 expired approximately two months ago.

Your conveyancer must request a **fresh Register Search** from the vendor's conveyancer (Sargeants Bayside / Kim Reed) before you sign anything. This protects you from encumbrances or dealings registered between September 2025 and now.

## Document 2: Adams Consulting Engineers — Structural Condition Report (Rev B, 20 April 2026)

Prepared by Curtis Young (Associate), inspection conducted 19 February 2026. This is the most authoritative document on the floor issue.

**Building description:**
- Constructed **around 2018** — approximately 6–7 years old
- Strip footings with bored piers; stumps/brick piers supporting bearers and joists
- Structural drawings reviewed (Simon Anderson Consultants)
- Original soil report (Structerre, December 2016) and a geotechnical report (Provincial Geotechnical Pty Ltd) were referenced

**Observed conditions:**
- Floor level difference: **~40mm across the living room, one side only** — not whole-building as Jim's B&P implied
- Remainder of structure is generally level
- **No significant cracking or structural distress** in walls, ceilings, or external cladding
- Doors and windows operational with no notable distortion
- Only minor cracking to block subfloor — rated "slight" per AS2870 (expected, not alarming)
- Geotechnical engineer could not confirm bored piers achieved adequate founding depth into weathered rock

**Structural risk assessment:**
- "No evidence was found to indicate significant structural concern"
- "The dwelling appears stable and serviceable in its current condition"
- Movement is "minor and not currently progressive"
- Classification: **Serviceability issue, not structural failure**

**Most probable cause:**
Either (a) construction tolerances when stumps were installed in 2018 — stumps slightly imperfect at build time — or (b) localised settlement from uncertain founding depth. If piers are founded in weathered rock as designed, construction tolerance is the most likely cause and the issue is not ongoing.

**Recommendations:**
- Re-levelling of existing brick piers/stumps (cheaper, but may crack walls; requires 1 year monitoring)
- OR underpinning (definitive fix; no monitoring required after)
- Minor finishes repair (plasterboard patching) after works

**Urgency:** Short term, not an immediate safety emergency.

**Limitation:** Geotechnical engineer could not verify founding depth of bored piers — the remaining uncertainty.

**Verdict on the floor issue:** Meaningfully more reassuring than Jim's B&P. Likely a build defect from 2018, not ongoing geological movement. Fixable for $10,900 with reasonable confidence.
